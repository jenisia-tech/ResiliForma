--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Hello everyone. Welcome to the demonstration of ResiliForma for Smart India 
Hackathon 2026 under Problem Statement SIH26114.

In smart city planning, architects and urban planners design their initial site 
in Autodesk Forma. Usually, they create two proposals: a conventional Baseline 
Proposal and a Resilient Proposal.

Our testbed is the Karunya Nagar Smart City Sector in Coimbatore—a 1.2 square 
kilometer master plan with 26 building blocks situated right along the busy 
NH-544 arterial highway."


[0:50 - 1:50] PART 2: WHAT RESILIFORMA DOES — CHECKING IF IT'S A GOOD PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(ACTION: Click [Baseline Proposal]. Point to the 2.5D SVG Canvas with the 26 
 buildings and the red/amber hazard cards below.)

"So what does our website do? 

ResiliForma takes the site design and screens it against real-world environmental 
hazards—highway traffic noise, solar sun heat, and monsoon rain runoff—to tell the 
planner whether it is a good plan or not.

Looking at the Baseline Proposal, the website immediately identifies that it is 
NOT a good plan:
• Traffic noise from the highway produces a severe 78 dBA across buildings.
• Direct western sun glare generates 710 Watts per square meter of intense heat load.
• Impervious hardscapes manage only 10% of heavy monsoon rain runoff.

This gives the baseline a low composite score of just 48 out of 100, or Grade D."


[1:50 - 3:00] PART 3: GENERATING THE OPTIMIZED RESILIENT PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(ACTION: Click [Resilient Proposal] or move the live sliders: Barrier to 3.5m, 
 Vegetation to 8m, Louver to 1.2m, Orientation to -18°. Watch the SVG canvas 
 update live with the berm, trees, rotated parcels, bioswales, and retention pond.)

"Since the baseline plan fails these environmental checks, ResiliForma generates 
an optimized plan built according to all the required site conditions.

The planner can explore or apply these interventions directly through our controls:
• We add a 3.5-meter acoustic earth berm and an 8-meter native tree buffer, cutting 
  noise by 15 dB down to 62.94 dBA for Improved Exposure.
• We rotate building façades by -18 degrees and add 1.2-meter shading louvers, 
  reducing direct solar heat by 32% down to 482 Watts per square meter.
• For heavy monsoon rainfall, we integrate 644 meters of bioswales and a 7,800 cubic 
  meter retention pond, raising stormwater management to 82%.

With these interventions, ResiliForma elevates the site to a Grade A score of 
87 out of 100—an improvement of +39 resilience points!"


[3:00 - 3:50] PART 4: PROPOSAL COMPARISON MATRIX

(ACTION: Scroll down to the Proposal Comparison Matrix table. Highlight the

"ResiliForma gives planners a side-by-side Proposal Comparison Matrix. It shows

This gives city planners transparent, mathematical proof to justify why the 
resilient plan is better before construction begins. This comparison aligns

[3:50 - 5:00] PART 5: FORMA INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE & 3D GENERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(ACTION: Scroll to the Forma Integration Panel showing the Adapter Flow and 
 8/8 Verified Checklist, then switch tab to the Autodesk Forma 3D site model.)

"Now, how does ResiliForma connect to Autodesk Forma?

Under the hood, ResiliForma uses a decoupled Adapter Pattern. For our demonstration, 
it runs in a zero-dependency Demo Mode with pre-normalized Karunya Nagar site context. 
This guarantees 100% reliable local evaluation without depending on cloud API tokens.

In production, this same adapter embeds directly inside Autodesk Forma via its Web SDK 
iframe, reading live 3D parcel selections automatically.

Once the user selects the optimal resilient plan from our website, Autodesk Forma 
generates the complete 3D site design step-by-step. 

Forma remains the primary platform where native simulations—like Sun Hours, Daylight, 
Wind Microclimate, and Embodied Carbon—are verified and exported to Revit BIM.

ResiliForma ensures planners make climate-resilient decisions from day one.

Thank you!"
